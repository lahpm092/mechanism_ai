# Map of Contents: Metabolic Connections

This MOC tracks the metabolic connections of the neuroendocrine-immune interface. The metabolic domain is a future expansion area; currently it captures the metabolic nodes that interact with the HPA axis and immune system.

## Steroid Metabolism

- [[cortisol]] — Primary glucocorticoid (metabolic effects: gluconeogenesis, lipolysis, protein catabolism)
- [[11beta-hsd1]] — Tissue-specific cortisol amplification (liver, adipose, brain)
- [[11beta-hsd2]] — Cortisol inactivation (kidney, placenta)

## Signaling Cascades with Metabolic Implications

- [[camp]] — Second messenger in steroidogenesis and metabolic signaling
- [[pka]] — Effector kinase for metabolic enzyme regulation
- [[star-protein]] — Cholesterol transport (rate-limiting for steroidogenesis)

## Metabolic Effects of Cortisol (Future Expansion)

The following connections are well-established but not yet modeled as mechanism files:

- Cortisol promotes hepatic gluconeogenesis via PEPCK and G6Pase induction
- Cortisol promotes lipolysis in peripheral adipose tissue
- Cortisol promotes protein catabolism in muscle
- Cortisol promotes visceral fat accumulation (via 11β-HSD1 in omental adipose)
- Chronic cortisol elevation drives insulin resistance

## Metabolic-Immune Crosstalk (Future Expansion)

- Metabolic syndrome is associated with chronic low-grade inflammation ([[nf-kb]] activation in adipose tissue)
- Visceral adipose macrophages ([[macrophage]]) produce [[tnf-alpha]] and [[il-6]]
- Insulin resistance alters immune cell metabolism and function
